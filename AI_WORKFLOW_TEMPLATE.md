# AI workflow

Delete these prompts and keep your answers brief. We care about what you directed and verified, not the volume of AI output.

## Tools used

- Name the AI tools or agents you used, if any.

## Two or three useful interactions

For each, say what you asked the tool to do, why you chose that task, what it produced, and how you decided whether to use it. Short excerpts are fine; full transcripts are unnecessary.

## One correction or rejection

- Describe one output you changed or rejected. What was wrong, and how did you notice?
- If you did not encounter one, describe a risk you checked for and the result.

## Verification

- What did you run or inspect yourself before submission?
- What remains unverified?

If you did not use AI, describe your development and verification workflow instead.
